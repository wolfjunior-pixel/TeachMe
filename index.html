Practise with the real exam

G.C.E. O/L past papers, mapped question by question

The six English-medium O/L ICT papers, 2020–2025, from the ictfromabc.com paper library. Every paper was read in full: open a question map and each question ID — Paper I Q1, Paper II Q3 — sits beside the chapter it tests. Check yourself

A short quiz for every chapter

Four questions per chapter, taken from the real 2020–2025 O/L papers — every answer names the paper and question it came from, so you can open that PDF and see it in place. Your best score per chapter is kept.

Ask the TA

Stuck on something? Ask away.

This helper answers from the lesson units in this app — try “binary”, “mail merge”, “SDLC” or “primary key”. For anything wider, it will point you to the chapter to read.

Built on the NIE Teacher’s Guides for ICT, Grades 10–11 (effective 2015/2016). Past papers: G.C.E. O/L ICT, English medium, 2020–2025, from the ictfromabc.com paper library — each card links directly to its PDF. Static edition: deploy this single file to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages as-is.
